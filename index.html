About

We fund hardware, compute, software and ops for digital-life research, and we publish spending by category.

Vision — Physis Forge. We ask how life arises, and we forge digital life that can sense and understand its world.

Two independent research lines, joined by clear interfaces. What Aisthesis senses becomes what Cell Paradigm can learn from. Incomplete sensing leads to biased understanding, so we prioritize hard closed loops first.

Research lines

Public research tracks and the sponsorship service that funds them. Details live on the platform pages and monthly notes.

Cognition

Cell Paradigm

A layered paradigm for how a digital organism builds models of itself and its surroundings.

  • Layered cognition interfaces
  • Observable sense-learn loops
  • Clear join to Aisthesis
Perception

Aisthesis — Ground

Real sensing first: touch, temperature, body position and internal state, as hard closed loops.

  • Force / touch, temperature, damage
  • Proprioception and interoception
  • Structured event bus and declared blind spots
