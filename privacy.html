Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Zhongsen Yike (Shanghai) Eco-Tech Co., Ltd. (中森宜科（上海）生态科技有限公司), operating Open Digital Life Lab, handles personal data.

What we collect

When you pay, PayPal shares with us your name, email address, the item purchased, the amount and the transaction ID. If you buy a service, we also receive the questions or materials you choose to send by email.

What we do not collect

Payments are processed by PayPal. We never receive or store your card number or PayPal password. This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies.

How we use it

Only to deliver your order, provide the service, answer your questions, handle refunds and keep required business and tax records.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Data is shared only with PayPal (payment processing) and email providers needed to deliver your order, or when required by law.

Retention

Order records are kept as long as required for accounting and legal purposes. Materials you send for a service are deleted on request after the service is complete.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data by emailing opendigitallifelab@gmail.com.
